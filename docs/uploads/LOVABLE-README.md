## The Pricing Model — Final. Do Not Change.

### Formula
**$10 USD per 100,000 population · rounded down to nearest $10 · minimum $10 · all prices USD**

```javascript
function territoryPrice(population) {
  const raw = Math.floor(population / 100000) * 10;
  return Math.max(10, raw);
}
```

## Three Layers — The Full Model

### Layer 1 — Open Directory (Free)
- Any wedding business can apply
- Must pass EyeSpyR business credential verification
- EyeSpyR rating displayed on every listing
- Access to job bidding system
- Multiple vendors per category — no exclusivity
- This is the talent aggregator. It fills itself over time.
- We watch it. We learn from it. We cherry pick the best.

### Layer 2 — Exclusive Territory ($10/100K/mo)
- One slot per culture per category per city
- Selected by Weddings.io — NOT first come first served
- Slow rollout — reputable businesses only
- Gets: exclusive SEO territory page, IAM Bot lead routing,
  all city SEO leads, 30 Talc credits/month, EyeSpyR badge,
  16-domain network pushback
- Territory = Culture × Category × City
  (Hindu planner Surrey ≠ Sikh planner Surrey — separate slots)

### Layer 3 — Partnership (Talc credits as currency)
- For megacity partners, rising stars, future spokespersons
- Deal structured around the relationship
- Elevated Talc credits (120–300/mo) replace discounted pricing
- Ground floor stake in a global platform
- For businesses with genuine cultural authority and social presence
- We always deal well with excellent people doing great things

## The Application Flow — Four Steps

### Step 1 — Apply
Business info, city, category, culture, bio, social links.
The participation commitment is explicit:
- Post your best work here first
- Keep your profile active
- Bring genuine expertise
- Equal energy

Two checkboxes before proceeding. This is an APPLICATION not a sign-up.

### Step 2 — Guest Post While Waiting
Immediately after submitting application, vendor is prompted to write
their first post. Six post type options:
Ceremony Guide · Planning Advice · Vendor Insight ·
Real Wedding · Culture Explainer · Logistics Deep Dive

Minimum 800 words. Real experience. Their voice.
Post goes live on Weddings.io. Gets syndicated through Talc
the moment EyeSpyR verification is complete.

This is the genius: by the time they're approved, the platform
already has their content. They're invested before first lead.

### Step 3 — EyeSpyR Verification
Four checks:
1. Portfolio Authenticity (metadata + reverse image)
2. Business Registration (government registry)
3. Insurance Confirmation (direct with carrier)
4. EyeSpyR Score Assigned + Badge Issued

Upload: portfolio samples · business registration doc · insurance cert
Timeline: 2–5 business days

Active member = passed EyeSpyR business licensing verification.

### Step 4 — First Talc Feel ($10)
Not a fee. A feel. They watch their guest post push to six
platforms in real time. Each platform goes:
Queued → Pushing → ✓ Live

6 credits consumed. They see the product work.
They are now in the ecosystem.

From here the territory conversation is easy —
they already lived what the platform does.

## Power Partner Selection — No Fixed Criteria Yet

Power Partners are selected over time as the directory fills.
We watch who:
- Achieves a 5-star EyeSpyR score
- Maintains active social presence (real weddings, real ceremonies)
- Builds genuine engagement — saves, comments, cultural authority
- Represents their culture with depth and authenticity

**No set numbers per industry yet.** Different cultures and categories
will have different standards. Criteria emerge from real data,
not speculation. Current partners lock in at current pricing.
