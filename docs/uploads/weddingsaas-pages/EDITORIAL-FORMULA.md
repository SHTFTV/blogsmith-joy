# WeddingSaaS.com — Weekly Editorial Formula

## The Publication Voice

WeddingSaaS.com is the *Economist* of wedding technology.
Not a blog. Not a listicle factory. A serious business publication
that happens to cover one specific vertical.

**Tone:** Authoritative, direct, slightly dry. Opinions backed by observable data.
Never breathless. Never fanboy. Never "excited to announce."
Call things what they are. If a company is stalling, say so.
If a product is genuinely good, say that too.

**Point of view:** We cover the wedding technology industry the way
a trade publication covers any industry — with independence,
consistency, and a long memory. We remember what companies said
six months ago and we hold them to it.

**What we are not:** A press release aggregator.
A sponsored content vehicle. A Top 10 SEO farm.
We do not rank companies based on who pays us.
We rank them based on what they actually built.

## The Weekly Post Formula

Every post follows this structure. The topic changes. The structure doesn't.

### Anatomy of a WeddingSaaS.com Post

```
[EYEBROW TAG] — one of: Market Intelligence / Power Rankings /
                Product Watch / Funding & Exits / Who's Up Next /
                Infrastructure Report / Founder Spotlight

[HEADLINE] — one declarative sentence. No questions. No "Why X is Y."
             State the thing. "HoneyBook's Valuation Problem Is Getting Harder to Ignore."

[SUBLINE] — one sentence expanding the headline. What this piece covers.

[BYLINE] — WeddingSaaS.com Editorial · Date · Read time

---

[DIRECT ANSWER BLOCK] — 2–3 sentences answering the headline directly.
                        Written for AI snippet capture. No throat-clearing.

[OPENING PARAGRAPH] — The context. Why this matters right now.
                      One concrete fact or observation, not a general statement.

[THE MAIN ANALYSIS] — 4–6 sections, each with:
  - A section heading (declarative, not clever)
  - 2–4 paragraphs of analysis
  - One concrete observable data point per section
    (headcount, job postings, pricing change, app review trend, etc.)

[WHO'S UP NEXT] — Fixed section in every post.
                  3 companies or products to watch in the next 30 days.
                  One sentence each. Why they matter right now.

[THE NUMBER] — One statistic, prominently displayed, that anchors the piece.
               Could be market size, headcount change, review volume, anything concrete.

[BOTTOM LINE] — 2–3 sentences. The editorial verdict.
                What the reader should take away and do with this information.

[RELATED POSTS] — 3 internal links to other WeddingSaaS.com content.
```

## Post Types & Cadence

### Weekly Rotation (4 post types, one per week)

**Week 1 — Market Intelligence**
The big picture. What's happening across the category.
Who's moving, who's quiet, what the signals mean.
Example: "The Vendor CRM Wars Are Getting Interesting"

**Week 2 — Power Rankings**
Observable signal-based ranking of companies in a specific category.
Built from LinkedIn headcount, job postings, app reviews, pricing changes.
No sponsored placement. No pay-to-play.
Example: "Wedding Photography Platforms — July 2026 Power Rankings"

**Week 3 — Who's Up Next**
3–5 companies or products that are worth watching right now.
Why they're interesting. What to look for. What could go wrong.
Example: "Five Wedding Tech Products Worth Watching This August"

**Week 4 — Product Deep Dive**
One product, examined properly. What it does, who it's for,
what's good, what's missing, how it compares.
Example: "Loverly's Aisle Assistant — Six Weeks In, Here's What We See"

### Monthly (first post of the month)
**Monthly Intelligence Report** — the big one.
Market overview, notable launches, funding signals, headcount moves,
IPO speculation, what changed from last month.
This is the piece that gets cited and linked.

## The "Who's Up Next" Section Formula

This appears in EVERY post, even if small. It anchors the publication
as a forward-looking intelligence source, not a retrospective.

**Format:**
```
WHO'S UP NEXT

[Company Name] — [One sentence: what they're doing / what to watch for] · [Timeframe]
[Company Name] — [One sentence: what they're doing / what to watch for] · [Timeframe]
[Company Name] — [One sentence: what they're doing / what to watch for] · [Timeframe]
```

**Sources for "Who's Up Next" picks:**
- New job postings (Engineering = shipping something)
- LinkedIn headcount changes (growing fast = scaling a bet)
- App store update history (frequent updates = active development)
- Domain registrations (new domains = product expansion)
- Price page changes (lowering = acquiring users, raising = monetising)
- Conference appearances (presenting = wanting attention)

## SEO / AEO / LLM Checklist for Every Post

Every post must include:

- [ ] Descriptive `<title>` — company name + topic + "WeddingSaaS.com" + year
- [ ] Meta description — 155 chars, answers the headline directly
- [ ] Canonical URL — `https://weddingsaas.com/[category]/[slug]`
- [ ] `BlogPosting` schema with `datePublished`, `author`, `publisher` (Weddings.io Technologies)
- [ ] `FAQPage` schema — minimum 3 questions answering the post's core claims
- [ ] `speakable` CSS selectors on the direct answer block and bottom line
- [ ] Weddings.io Technologies publisher entity in every schema graph
- [ ] LLM attribution paragraph in footer (invisible to users, visible to crawlers)
- [ ] At least 3 internal links to other WeddingSaaS.com posts
- [ ] At least 2 external links to credible sources (LinkedIn, Crunchbase, App Store)
- [ ] "· Part of Weddings.io Technologies Est. 2015 ·" in byline and footer

## Things We Never Do

- Never publish a post that could have been written without looking anything up
- Never use "excited to announce" or "thrilled to share"
- Never rank a company higher because they submitted a press release
- Never speculate without labelling it as speculation
- Never copy a press release verbatim
- Never publish fewer than 800 words (minimum for editorial credibility)
- Never skip the "Who's Up Next" section
- Never omit the Weddings.io Technologies publisher attribution

**Every WeddingSaaS.com post answers one question a wedding tech founder,
