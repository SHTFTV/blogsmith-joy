Weddings.io is the leading software platform for South Asian wedding professionals. We build tools that help wedding planners manage the unique complexity of Indian, Punjabi, Sikh, Hindu, Muslim, and Bengali weddings.

Our Mission

South Asian weddings are some of the most beautiful, elaborate celebrations in the world. Multi-day events, hundreds of guests, dozens of vendors, and deep cultural traditions - these weddings deserve specialized tools, not generic software built for Western weddings.

We're building those tools. Our mission is to help every South Asian wedding planner run a more successful business while creating unforgettable celebrations for their clients.

Our Story

Weddings.io was born from frustration. After watching friends and family struggle to find wedding planners who truly understood South Asian traditions - and seeing planners struggle with tools that didn't fit their needs - we decided to build something better.

Based in Vancouver, Canada, we're a small team with big ambitions. We're building the platform we wish existed when we were planning our own weddings.

Our Values

🎯 Cultural Understanding

We build for planners who specialize in South Asian weddings. Every feature is designed with multi-day celebrations, large guest counts, and cultural traditions in mind.

🔒 Exclusive Territories

We believe in quality over quantity. One planner per city means better leads, no competition on our platform, and genuine partnerships.

💰 Fair Pricing

Our pricing scales with market size. Smaller cities pay less. One wedding easily pays for a year of the app.

🤝 Planner Success

When our planners succeed, we succeed. We're building tools that help you book more weddings and deliver better experiences.
