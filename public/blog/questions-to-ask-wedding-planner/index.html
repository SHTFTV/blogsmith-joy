HIRING GUIDE

25 Questions to Ask Before Hiring a Wedding Planner

8 min read • Updated January 2026

Hiring a wedding planner is one of the most important decisions you'll make. For South Asian weddings, it's crucial to find someone who understands the complexity of multi-day celebrations, cultural traditions, and large guest counts.

Use these 25 questions during your consultations to find the right fit.

Experience & Background

1How many South Asian weddings have you planned?
Look for specific experience, not just "multicultural" weddings.
2Have you planned [Hindu/Sikh/Muslim/Bengali] weddings specifically?
Each tradition has unique requirements. Make sure they know yours.
3Can you share photos/videos from similar weddings you've planned?
Portfolio proof matters more than promises.
4Can I speak with past South Asian clients as references?
Real feedback from couples with similar needs.

Understanding Your Traditions

5What do you know about [specific ceremony, e.g., Anand Karaj, pheras, nikah]?
Test their actual knowledge, not just claims of expertise.
6How would you coordinate a baraat procession?
Dhol, horse, timing, venue coordination - it's complex.
7Have you worked with pandits/granthis/imams before?
Religious officiants have specific needs.
8How do you handle multi-day wedding timelines?
Mehendi, Sangeet, Wedding, Reception - all need coordination.

Logistics & Vendors

9Do you have relationships with South Asian vendors (caterers, decorators, DJs)?
Good vendor relationships = better service and possibly better rates.
10Can you recommend Indian caterers who can handle 500+ guests?
Large-scale South Asian catering is specialized.
11How do you handle dietary restrictions across multiple events?
Vegetarian, Jain, halal, allergies - it's a lot to manage.
12Do you have experience with mandap/stage design and setup?
Not all decorators understand mandap requirements.

Working Style & Communication

13How many weddings do you take on per weekend?
If they're double-booked, you won't get full attention.
14Will you personally be at all our events, or will it be a team member?
Know who you're actually working with.
15How do you prefer to communicate (email, phone, app)?
Make sure your styles are compatible.
16How often will we have check-in meetings?
Regular communication prevents surprises.

Budget & Contracts

17What are your packages and pricing?
Understand exactly what's included and what costs extra.
18Do you charge per event or for the entire wedding?
Multi-day weddings can be priced differently.
19What's your payment schedule?
Know what's due when.
20What's your cancellation/refund policy?
Hopefully you won't need it, but know it anyway.
21Do you receive commissions from vendors you recommend?
Transparency matters for trust.

Problem-Solving

22What's the biggest challenge you've faced at a South Asian wedding and how did you handle it?
Real problems reveal real skills.
23How do you handle last-minute family drama or changes?
It happens. You need someone calm under pressure.
24What's your backup plan if a key vendor cancels?
Contingency planning is essential.
25How do you manage family members who want to be very involved?
South Asian weddings often have many cooks in the kitchen.

Red Flags to Watch For

Be cautious if a planner:

  • Can't name specific South Asian weddings they've planned
  • Doesn't know basic terms (mandap, baraat, sangeet)
  • Seems unfamiliar with your religious/cultural traditions
  • Is vague about pricing or contracts
  • Won't provide references from past clients
  • Is taking on too many weddings around your date

Trust your instincts. You'll be working closely with this person for months - make sure it's someone you genuinely like and trust.
