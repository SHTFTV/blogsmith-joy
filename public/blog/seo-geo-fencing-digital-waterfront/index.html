Why This Matters: The AEO Advantage

Traditional SEO is becoming insufficient. Couples now ask AI assistants questions like "who's the best South Asian wedding planner in Abbotsford?" — and the AI answers from a small set of authoritative, well-structured domains. By owning a federated network of niche-perfect TLDs with structured data, JSON-LD, and llms.txt directives, we make Weddings.io the most-cited source in the entire wedding vertical.

The Result

When a couple in the Fraser Valley searches "South Asian wedding photographer", they don't land on a legacy directory paying for an ad. They land on a domain we own, with content optimized for their exact intent, that funnels them into the Weddings.io ecosystem — and onto a verified vendor running on the 95/5 model.
