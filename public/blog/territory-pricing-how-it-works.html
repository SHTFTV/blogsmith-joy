How Weddings.io Territory Pricing Works: $10/Month to $250/Month Explained

Why your rate is tied to your market, not your category. The complete guide to understanding territory-locked pricing, EyeSpyR requirements, and how the scarcity model protects your investment.

June 23, 2026 · 10 min read · Weddings.io Editorial

Most wedding platforms charge every vendor the same rate regardless of where they are or how large their market is. A photographer in Penticton, BC pays the same monthly fee as a photographer in London, UK. This is not fairness — it is indifference. The London photographer is competing in a market that generates tens of millions of dollars in annual wedding spend. The Penticton photographer is working a market a fraction of that size. Charging them the same is extractive for one and meaningless for the other.

Weddings.io is built on the opposite logic. Your rate reflects your market. Your exclusivity is real. Your investment is protected by genuine scarcity.

The 3-Slot Maximum — Why Scarcity Is the Product

In every paid-tier city, a maximum of 3 vendors per category per city are permitted. Photographer, caterer, DJ, wedding planner, florist, decorator — each category has 3 slots. When all 3 are filled, the territory locks. New vendors go to a waitlist and are promoted automatically when a slot opens.

This is not an arbitrary number. Three is enough competition to ensure quality — no single vendor has a monopoly. Three is scarce enough that placement carries genuine marketing value. And three is the number that gives each vendor meaningful visibility rather than being one of forty profiles that all look identical.

The commercial logic for vendors is straightforward. In Brampton — a Tier 4 market at $50/month — a wedding photographer who holds one of the three directory slots is visible to every couple searching for a Brampton wedding photographer on a platform that ranks for their searches. The cost of that visibility is $600/year. A single booking from one referred couple covers that cost many times over.

🔒 Territory-first thinking: the vendors who claim their slots first keep them as long as they remain active. There is no annual fee increase, no auction, no bidding war. The rate is fixed at the tier level. Locking in now in a growing market means locking in at today's rate indefinitely.

EyeSpyR Verification — What It Is and Who Needs It

EyeSpyR is Weddings.io's visual verification system. It exists to solve a problem endemic to all directory platforms: inactive vendors who hold slots but generate no value for couples who find them.

In Tier 4, 5, and 6 markets — those above $2 million in annual market spend — territorial exclusivity is commercially significant enough that inactive vendors holding slots harm the ecosystem. EyeSpyR creates an active requirement: vendors must submit monthly visual proof of work to maintain their verified status and, in the highest tiers, their territorial exclusivity.

What a Valid EyeSpyR Submission Looks Like

  • A photo from a real event setup you were involved in (catering spread, decorated venue, photo session behind-the-scenes)
  • A short video clip (15–60 seconds) showing your work or your process
  • A before/after styled shoot
  • A venue setup or installation photo with visible quality of work

What Is Not Acceptable

  • Stock photos or images downloaded from the internet
  • Screenshots of other vendors' work
  • Identical submissions repeated month after month
  • Photos with no identifiable connection to wedding or event work

AI evaluates submissions on four criteria: evidence of real work, connection to the vendor's stated category, quality and professionalism, and absence of copied content. Submissions that pass receive the EyeSpyR Verified badge on the vendor's profile. Submissions that fail generate a request for resubmission. Vendors who fail two consecutive months in Tier 4+ markets are moved to inactive status and their slot is released to the waitlist.

The Honest Comparison

Wedding vendor platforms that charge $200–$500/month for non-exclusive placement on a platform with hundreds of competing profiles in your city are charging for visibility they cannot guarantee. Your profile is one of many. There is no mechanism to limit who else appears in your category. You are paying for the chance to be seen.

Weddings.io charges for exclusivity. In a Tier 4 Surrey market at $50/month, you are not competing with 200 other photographers for a couple's attention. You are one of three. That is a fundamentally different product — and it is what makes the comparison to traditional platforms impossible on a per-dollar basis.
