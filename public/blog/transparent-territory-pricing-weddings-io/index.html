Transparent Territory Pricing: The Weddings.io Bracket Model

Every bracket published. Flat $10 per 100K population, up to $290/mo at 29M+. No sales calls, no hidden tiers.

July 12, 2026 • 6 min • Weddings.io Editorial

Most marketplaces price local vendor placement behind a sales call. Weddings.io does the opposite: the entire territory pricing matrix is published, hardcoded, and identical for every vendor in a given city.

The Model in One Sentence

Each territory has a fixed number of slots and a flat monthly rate per slot, both determined by the city's population. There is no interpolation, no negotiation, and no dynamic surge pricing.

The Bracket Structure

  • Baseline (up to 2M population): $10/slot/month.
  • Growth brackets (2M → 29M): price rises $10 for each additional million residents.
  • Ceiling (29M+): $290/slot/month — the maximum any vendor ever pays.
  • Total brackets: 39, all defined in TERRITORY_MATRIX.

Add-Ons — Also Flat

  • Position #1 lock: +50% of the active slot rate.
  • Backlink Pack: $25 one-time for 3 dofollow links.
  • TALC.tv distribution: $10/post.
  • EyeSpyR Hall Visualizer: $2/render (free monthly with any $10/mo+ plan).
  • Guest Post: $10 per accepted post.

Why Publish It All?

Transparent pricing removes the two failure modes of local ad marketplaces: opaque bidding and slot inflation. Vendors know exactly what a territory costs before they ever create an account, and a bracket is only marked sold out when its exact slot count is filled.

How to Check Your City

Open the pricing page, type your city's population, and the displayed rate updates instantly. The same number appears in checkout, on invoices, and in every renewal — including PayPal payments.
