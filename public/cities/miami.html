Why South Asian Wedding Planners Need Specialized Software

South Asian weddings are fundamentally different from Western weddings. They typically span multiple days with events like Mehendi, Sangeet, Haldi, Pithi, Baraat, the main ceremony (whether Hindu Vivah, Sikh Anand Karaj, Muslim Nikah, or other traditions), and Reception. Guest counts of 300-1000+ are common. This complexity requires specialized tools that generic wedding planning software doesn't provide.
