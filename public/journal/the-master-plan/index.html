Why weddings first

Weddings are where the stakes are highest and the trust failures are most painful. A couple spends months planning the most important day of their lives, hires vendors based on reviews that may be fake, and has no recourse when something goes wrong. The wedding industry runs on word of mouth and reputation — and yet has almost no reliable infrastructure for verifying either.

That gap is where Weddings.io sits. Not as another directory. As a territory platform where one verified expert holds the exclusive slot for each city — selected by us, not by who paid first. Where every review is traced to a real transaction. Where every dispute has a documented arbitration process. Where the AI surfaces the best talent before you even know to look for it.

When the infrastructure works here — in an industry this emotional, this high-stakes, this dependent on trust — it works anywhere. The 100+ industry categories in the IAM network are the proof of that.

EyeSpyR — the trust layer the industry was missing

There is no reliable way to verify a wedding professional right now. Reviews can be purchased. Portfolios can be borrowed. Business registrations can be recent. The couple booking a photographer has no way to know whether that photographer has ever actually delivered the photos they promised.

EyeSpyR changes that at the infrastructure level. Every business on the Weddings.io network goes through EyeSpyR before they are listed. The AI checks public records, complaint databases, court filings, bad press, and the network's own red flag database — built from every failed application across all 100+ IAM domains. A clean record starts at 4.5 stars. Something bad found — no listing, no charge, no hard feelings.

After entry, the EyeSpyR score moves in real time. Every verified review — traced to an actual transaction in the platform — carries full weight. An anonymous review with no transaction behind it carries zero. The AI weights the score continuously based on behaviour, engagement, response rates, and review velocity. The floater badge on every listing reflects the actual current reputation of that business, not a static score from a one-time check.

Every review on Weddings.io is traceable. Every dispute has a process. We do what the other platforms won't.

EyeSpyR is also a SaaS tool in its own right — available to any platform or marketplace that needs business verification at scale. The same intelligence that protects Weddings.io couples can protect customers in any industry. That is the larger vision.

Talc — AI content from a verified source

AI-generated content is everywhere. Most of it is generic, unverifiable, and increasingly invisible to search engines that have learned to discount it. The problem is not AI — it is AI with no identity behind it.

Talc solves that by anchoring AI content to a verified business identity. When a wedding planner in Surrey uses Talc to write about Haldi ceremony traditions, that content is attributed to a real, EyeSpyR-verified business with a real track record. The AI helps write it. The AI reformats it for each platform. But the source is verified — and that verification is what makes the content trustworthy to both couples and search engines.

One post through Talc goes to Google Business, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, and an SEO blog post — simultaneously, reformatted for each platform's specific requirements. The Instagram caption reads like Instagram. The LinkedIn post reads like LinkedIn. The Google Business update reads like a local business update. The AI handles the translation. The verified professional provides the expertise.

Territory holders on Weddings.io get Talc credits bundled with their monthly fee — one credit per $10 spent. A Surrey planner at $50 a month gets five posts across six platforms every month. That is thirty pieces of platform-native content per month, all attributed to a verified local expert, all feeding the territory page's authority.

For everyone else — any wedding professional anywhere in the world — Talc is available publicly at $10 per post. No membership required. No territory needed. Just a verified business identity and something worth saying.

One honest note: Talc works by connecting to the APIs of major social platforms. If those platforms change their policies or restrict third-party access — and some have, and some will — our access can be affected. We are always transparent when that happens. We are always building toward more platforms, more direct relationships, and a more resilient distribution network. The ecosystem is designed to evolve. New platforms, new channels, new distribution methods — if you have a product that fits, we want to hear from you.

A $10 annual listing does not cover infrastructure. A $10 minimum territory does not cover the SEO work we do to rank that page. We are investing in the platform's credibility before we ask anyone to invest in it.

The pricing also reflects something we believe is simply right — that geography should not determine whether a professional can afford to participate. As the network grows, the pricing reflects the value being created. A Toronto territory is worth more than a Langley territory because Toronto has ten times the population. The formula is public. You can calculate your own price before you ever speak to us.

The most inclusive wedding platform in the world

Most wedding platforms were built for North America and Europe. Their pricing assumes a certain economy. Their cultural categories assume a certain kind of wedding. Their verification systems assume a certain kind of business infrastructure. Everyone else is an afterthought — if they are a thought at all.

We built this differently from the beginning.

A wedding planner in Surrey BC and a wedding planner in Lagos Nigeria face the same fundamental challenge — couples who need to trust them, a reputation that needs to be verifiable, and content that needs to reach the right audience. The tools they need are the same. The price they can afford is not.

Our pricing is adjusted for local purchasing power using World Bank data — the same methodology used by economists to compare economies fairly across the globe. A $50 territory in Surrey reflects what $50 means in Surrey. A territory in a comparable Indian city is priced at what that same purchasing power means locally. Same product. Same EyeSpyR verification. Same Talc distribution. Same IAM Bot intelligence. Same standards. Priced fairly for where you actually live.

The formula is public. Transparent. Reviewable. It does not change based on negotiation or relationship or who you know. $10 per 100,000 population, adjusted for your country's price level, minimum $10. That is it. A wedding planner in a small city in Kenya pays the same minimum as a wedding planner in a small city in Canada. Because the minimum commitment to the platform should be the same everywhere — what that minimum costs in real terms should not be.

This is not charity. It is the only model that makes sense if you genuinely believe the best wedding professionals exist everywhere in the world — not just in the cities that historically had the money to be noticed. We believe that. We built the pricing to prove it.

The most inclusive wedding platform in the world is not a marketing line. It is a formula. Public, transparent, and the same for everyone.
