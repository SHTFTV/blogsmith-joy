Founding story

Weddings.io launched in 2015 as a counter-model to the dominant wedding directories. The Knot, WeddingWire, and Zola had built businesses by selling vendor placement and showing couples sponsored results. The result for couples was a discovery experience optimized for ad revenue, not match quality. The result for vendors was paying ever-rising fees to compete with everyone else on the same page.

Weddings.io replaced that model with territory locks — each city is sold to one vendor per category at $10 per 100,000 people in the market per month. Vendors get exclusivity. Couples get tools that are free forever and a directory where placement signals exclusivity, not ad spend. Premium ($9.99/month) and Pro ($19.99/month) plans exist only for genuinely server-side features: cloud sync, photo storage, multi-wedding dashboards.

The platform spans nine wedding traditions — South Asian, Chinese, Persian, Jewish, Mexican, Nordic, Southeast Asian, Western Contemporary, and Traditional & Religious — with culture-specific tools (Sofreh Aghd layouts, Hora chair calculators, Catholic Nuptial Mass timelines, Chinese tea ceremony scripts) that legacy directories do not offer.
