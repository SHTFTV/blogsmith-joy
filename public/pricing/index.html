Pricing FAQ

Is the couples free plan really free?

Yes. Every planning tool — budget calculator, guest list (up to 50), checklist, 2D/3D floor planner, vendor CRM (up to 10), invoice builder, AI chatbot, and all 9 culture tool pages — is free forever. No account, no credit card, no ads. Data saves to your browser only.

What does Couples Cloud add?

$4.99/month gets cloud sync across all your devices, 5GB of photo storage, unlimited guest list and vendor CRM, PDF export, partner/planner sharing, and 30-day automatic backup.

How does the 250 Scale work?

Every city starts at 3 slots minimum and is capped at 10. Population sets the price per slot per month: $10 under 1M, $20 at 1M–1.99M, $30 at 2M–2.99M, $40 at 3M–3.99M, $50 at 4M+. Sold out = waitlist only. Cancel anytime with 30 days notice.

How do I qualify for the Millionaires Club?

You qualify automatically when you lock a territory in a city with population over 1M. Silver (1M–1.99M) = 20% off any app tier. Gold (2M–2.99M) = 30% off. Platinum (3M–3.99M) = 40% off. Diamond (4M+) = 50% off. Discount activates when your territory tier is verified. Contact partnerships@industryarmymarketing.com.

How much is guest posting?

$10 one-time per accepted post. You get a permanent byline, one dofollow backlink in your author bio, one dofollow in body when contextually natural, and a permanent author profile at /contributors/[name]/.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Couples Cloud and Planner tiers cancel at the end of the billing cycle. Territory locks release with 30 days notice. The Backlink Package is a one-time charge with no renewal — links are permanent.

Do you show ads?

No. Ever. Vendor listings appear because the vendor locked the territory — never because they paid for ad placement.
