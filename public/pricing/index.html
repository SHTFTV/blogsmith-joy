Pricing FAQ

Is the free plan really free forever?

Yes. Every core planning tool — budget, guest list, checklists, vendor discovery, community, Day-Of task board — is free forever. No card. No login required. Vendors fund the platform, not couples.

What does the $99 Legacy Pass include?

One-time payment, lifetime access for couples. Adds the advanced budget planner, multi-event guest management, priority vendor matching, seating chart builder, vendor reviews, and Day-Of live coordination on top of everything in Free.

How does the $299/year Planners plan work?

Annual subscription for professional wedding planners. Includes everything in Couples plus unlimited client management, multi-wedding dashboard, team collaboration, white-label client portal, revenue analytics, priority support, and TALC.tv content credits.

How does Nano $2/viz pricing work?

Pay $2 per AI venue render. No subscription. Works on the free plan and every paid tier equally. Each render produces a realistic indoor or outdoor venue layout you can share with vendors.

Do you ever show ads?

No. Not on Free, not on Legacy Pass, not on Planners. Ever. Vendor listings appear because the vendor locked the territory — never because they paid for ad placement.

Can I cancel anytime?

Legacy Pass is one-time — nothing to cancel. The Planners annual plan can be cancelled from your account; you keep access through the end of the billing year, then data exports to a downloadable archive.
