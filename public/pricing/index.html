Pricing FAQ

Is the free plan really free?

Yes. Every essential planning tool — budget calculator, vendor discovery, checklists, community, Day-Of task board — is free forever. No card. No login required. Vendors fund the platform.

What does the $99 Legacy Pass include?

One-time payment, lifetime access. Advanced budget planner, multi-event guest management, priority vendor matching, seating chart builder, vendor reviews & comparisons, and Day-Of live coordination — on top of everything in Free.

How is vendor territory pricing calculated?

$10 per 100,000 population per month, with a $5/mo minimum floor for smaller cities. Toronto (2.8M) = $28/mo per category. Surrey (568K) = $57/mo. Abbotsford (186K) = $5/mo (minimum). Each of the 17 categories locks independently.

What's the difference between a Guest Post and a TALC.tv Blast?

Guest Posts ($10) publish your article to a specific city page with a permanent backlink. TALC.tv Blasts ($10) take one photo and AI-generate a 2,000-word blog post that gets pushed to weddings.io, Google My Business, X, Facebook, and Instagram — all GPS-verified by EyeSpyR.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Vendor territory locks bill monthly and release on next renewal. Planner plans renew annually. The Legacy Pass is a one-time charge with no renewal.

Do you ever show paid ads?

No. Vendor listings appear because the vendor locked the territory — never because they paid for ad placement. One vendor per category, per city. That's the whole model.
