🍜 10-Course Banquet Sequencer

Traditional Chinese wedding banquet course order with serving times and key logistics notes for your catering team.

1
Cold Appetizer Platter
Jellyfish, cold cuts, century egg, smoked duck — arrange at table before guests arrive
Serve on arrival · 45 min window
2
Braised Shark Fin Soup
Or premium substitute (crab meat + mushroom). First hot course — signals formal start
After couple's entrance
3
Whole Roasted Suckling Pig
Presented whole tableside — the centrepiece. Skin must be crisp, presented to hosts first
MC announces · Photo moment
4
Lobster or King Prawn
Whole lobster with ginger-scallion sauce, or prawns — symbolises happiness (虾 sounds like laughter)
20 min after suckling pig
5
Abalone & Sea Cucumber
Premium prestige course — signals family's generosity. Braised in oyster sauce
Couple table-toasting begins
6
Whole Steamed Garoupa
Must be served whole — head and tail intact, head facing most senior guest at table
Mid-banquet
7
Braised Fish Maw
Or braised mushroom with vegetables — symbolises prosperity
After fish course
8
Roasted Duck or Chicken
Whole bird presented — duck symbolises fidelity. Chicken for budget-conscious families
Speeches begin
9
Fried Rice or E-Fu Noodles
E-Fu (longevity noodles) never cut — long noodles symbolise long life together
Near end of dinner
10
Sweet Tong Sui + Pastries
Red bean soup or mango pudding · Wife cakes and sesame balls for tables · Wedding cake cutting
Final course · Cake cutting last
