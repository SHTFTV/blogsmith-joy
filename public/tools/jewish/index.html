🥘 Kosher Venue Verification Checklist

Questions to ask every venue before booking for a Kosher wedding. Check off each item as confirmed with the venue.

Mashgiach (Kosher Supervisor) Available

☐
Separate Meat & Dairy Preparation Areas
Physical separation of meat and dairy — separate prep surfaces, cookware, and utensils
☐
Kosher Certification (Hechsher) on All Ingredients

☐
Separate Meat & Dairy Dishwashing

☐

☐

🕍 Chuppah Positioning Guide

Key requirements for Chuppah placement — indoor vs outdoor, orientation, and structural considerations.

Indoor Chuppah

  • →Minimum ceiling height: 12 feet (standard); 14+ feet ideal for floral hanging
  • →Chuppah poles: 8–10 feet tall, 8×8 foot canopy minimum
  • →Face East (toward Jerusalem) if possible
  • →Clear aisle of 5 feet minimum for processional
  • →Space for Rabbi, couple, and 2 witnesses under canopy

Outdoor Chuppah

  • →Under open sky is traditional and preferred in many traditions
  • →Secure poles with sandbags or stakes — 25 lb minimum per pole
  • →Wind contingency: backup indoor location on standby
  • →Face guests with sun behind them (not in their eyes)
  • →Nighttime: add spotlights aimed at canopy, not into crowd
