📅 Two-Night Event Planner

Persian weddings often span two nights. The Aghd (legal ceremony) and the Jashn-e Aroosi (celebration party) may be held on different days. Plan each separately.

اقد Aghd Night — Typical Timing

The formal legal and religious ceremony. Held at home or small venue with close family.

4:00 PM
Sofreh Aghd table setup complete — flowers, items, candles lit
5:00 PM
Guests arrive — close family only (50–150 people)
6:00 PM
Bride enters — escorted by parents, seated at Sofreh Aghd
6:15 PM
Groom enters — seated opposite bride at Sofreh Aghd
6:30 PM
Aghd ceremony begins — mullah, rabbi, or officiant leads
6:45 PM
Needle and thread ceremony — Kalleh Ghand grinding
7:00 PM
Aqd Nameh (marriage contract) signed by both parties and witnesses
7:15 PM
Ring exchange, honey feeding, family congratulations
7:30 PM
Dinner, tea, sweets served to family
9:00 PM
Evening winds down — couple receives guests privately

Jashn-e Aroosi — Reception Night Timing

The main celebration party — large venue, live band or DJ, dinner and dancing for all guests.

6:00 PM
Doors open — guests arrive, cocktail hour with Persian appetizers
7:30 PM
Couple's grand entrance — live Persian music (tar + tombak)
7:45 PM
First dance — couple's first dance as married couple
8:00 PM
Dinner service begins — Persian rice (chelou), khoresh, kebabs
9:00 PM
Speeches and toasts — Farsi and English
9:30 PM
Dancing begins — Persian pop, band switches to dance set
10:30 PM
Cake cutting ceremony
11:00 PM
Open dance floor — DJ takes over, Persian and Western mix
12:30 AM
Last dance — couple's exit

🎶 Persian Live Music Scheduler

Traditional Persian instruments for ceremony and reception. Each has a different role and timing in the event.

Tar (تار)
Long-necked lute
The soul of Persian classical music. Used during the Aghd ceremony and for formal dinner hour. Melodic, evocative, emotionally powerful.
Aghd ceremony · Cocktail hour · Dinner
Santur (سنتور)
Hammered dulcimer
Bright, shimmering tone. Often paired with violin for classical Persian music. Popular for Aghd background music and first dance.
Aghd background · First dance
Tombak (تنبک)
Persian goblet drum
The primary Persian percussion. Drives the rhythm for both classical and dance music. Essential when shifting to dancing portion.
All dancing sets · Reception
Kamancheh (کمانچه)
Spike fiddle
Haunting, soulful bowed string instrument. Often featured during emotional moments — bride's entrance, ceremony, first dance.
Ceremony · Entrances
Daf (دف)
Frame drum
Large frame drum with jingles. Common in Sufi-influenced ceremonies and joyful processional moments. Creates communal energy.
Processional · Celebration
Persian DJ
Electronic / Pop mix
For the late-night dancing portion. Mix of Persian pop (Googoosh, Dariush, Shahram Nazeri), modern Persian EDM, and Western hits.
Post-midnight dance floor
