Traditional & Religious Wedding Planner

Catholic Nuptial Mass timelines, church music, Orthodox crowning, rehearsal dinner run-sheets, and traditional vow customisation — built for couples planning a sacred church wedding.

1. Catholic Nuptial Mass Timeline

A full Nuptial Mass with Eucharist runs 60–75 minutes. Use this structure with your celebrant 6–8 weeks before the wedding.

TimeRiteNotes
0:00Processional & Entrance HymnMothers seated, wedding party, bride enters. 6–8 min.
0:08Greeting & Opening PrayerSign of the Cross, collect prayer. 3 min.
0:11First Reading (Old Testament)Lector. Often Genesis 2 or Tobit 8. 3 min.
0:14Responsorial PsalmCantor. Psalm 128 standard. 3 min.
0:17Second Reading (Epistle)Lector. 1 Corinthians 13 most common. 3 min.
0:20Gospel Acclamation & GospelPriest/deacon reads. Matthew 19 or John 15. 4 min.
0:24HomilyPersonalised reflection. 6–10 min.
0:34Rite of MarriageAddress, consent, vows, ring exchange, declaration. 12 min.
0:46Liturgy of the EucharistPreparation of gifts, Eucharistic Prayer, Communion. 20–25 min.
1:08Nuptial Blessing & RecessionalFinal blessing, sign register, recessional hymn. 7 min.

2. Tools & Planners

Music

Church Music Scheduler

Cue sheet for organist, cantor and string ensemble across the four music moments.

  1. Processional: Canon in D · Trumpet Voluntary · Jesu Joy
  2. Offertory: Ave Maria · Panis Angelicus · How Great Thou Art
  3. Signing of Register: Pachelbel · The Prayer · Cantor solo
  4. Recessional: Mendelssohn Wedding March · Ode to Joy · Hornpipe
Procession

Flower Girl & Ring Bearer Coordinator

Age-appropriate roles, walking order, rehearsal prompts and backup plan.

  • Ideal age: 4–8 years (older flower girls = "junior bridesmaid")
  • Walk just before maid of honour (or just before bride)
  • Pre-walk practice at rehearsal — 3 full runs minimum
  • Quiet seat reserved in front pew with parent for after entrance
  • Backup: an aunt or grandparent shadows in case of tears
Rehearsal

Rehearsal Dinner Run-Sheet

Standard 3-hour run-sheet for the night before. Hosts: groom's parents traditionally.

  1. 6:00 — Arrival & cocktails
  2. 6:30 — Welcome by host parent
  3. 6:45 — Dinner served
  4. 7:30 — Toasts: parents, then maid of honour, then best man
  5. 8:15 — Couple's thank-you & gift presentation to wedding party
  6. 8:45 — Coffee, dessert, mingle
  7. 9:30 — Close. Wedding party home by 10:30.
Vows

Traditional Vow Customiser

Religious ceremonies require canonical vows. You may personalise within the structure.

  • Catholic standard: "I, ___, take you, ___, to be my wife/husband. I promise to be faithful to you, in good times and in bad, in sickness and in health, to love you and to honor you all the days of my life."
  • Anglican standard (BCP): "...to have and to hold from this day forward, for better for worse, for richer for poorer, in sickness and in health, to love and to cherish, till death us do part."
  • Personalised addition: permitted in form of a separate "ring blessing" sentence — clear with celebrant 4 weeks prior
Unity

Unity Candle / Sand Ceremony Tracker

Optional unity rite — placed after the vows, before the Eucharist (or before the recessional in a non-Mass ceremony).

  • Unity candle: two taper candles (lit by mothers during processional) → one pillar candle, lit together by couple
  • Sand ceremony: two coloured vials of sand → one vessel. Preferred outdoor / non-Catholic ceremonies.
  • Wine ceremony: shared cup; sometimes used in Anglican services
  • Equipment list: candles + lighter pre-lit, sand vials labelled, vessel pre-positioned on altar table
  • Time budget: 3 minutes
Orthodox

Crowning Ceremony Planner

For Greek, Russian, Serbian, Romanian, and Antiochian Orthodox weddings.

  1. Betrothal Service — rings exchanged 3x by priest
  2. Service of Crowning — stefana placed on couple by koumbaro
  3. Scripture readings (Ephesians 5, John 2)
  4. Common cup — shared 3 sips of wine
  5. Dance of Isaiah — three processions around the table
  6. Removal of crowns & final blessing
