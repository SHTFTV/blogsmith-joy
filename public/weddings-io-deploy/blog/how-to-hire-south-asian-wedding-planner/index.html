How to Hire a South Asian Wedding Planner: 2026 Guide

Questions to ask, fees to expect, contracts to sign, and the red flags worth walking away from.

6 min read • May 13, 2026 • By the Weddings.io Editorial Team

Hiring the right wedding planner is the single highest-leverage decision a South Asian couple makes. The right planner saves you 200+ hours, prevents six-figure mistakes, and absorbs the family politics. The wrong one becomes the disaster.

Step 1 — Decide what you actually need

Full planning, partial planning, or month-of coordination? A multi-day wedding with 400+ guests almost always needs full planning. A single reception with 120 guests can usually be run with month-of coordination plus strong vendors.

Step 2 — Shortlist verified planners only

Use the verified planner directory on Weddings.io to filter by city, event type, and budget. Every listed planner has completed KYC, has live client reviews, and a public track record of weddings delivered. Skip Instagram-only operators with no verifiable history.

Step 3 — Ask the right questions on the first call

How many South Asian weddings have you delivered in the last 24 months? What is your average couple budget? Who on your team is on-site each day? What happens if a key vendor cancels 14 days out? How do you handle family disagreements? Their answers should be specific, not generic.

Step 4 — Understand the fee structure

Most experienced South Asian planners charge 10–15% of total wedding spend, or a flat fee starting around $15,000 for full planning. Beware of planners who take undisclosed kickbacks from vendors — ask directly. Weddings.io operates on a 95/5 model, so verified planners on the platform never bury hidden commissions.

Step 5 — Sign a real contract

Scope of work, deliverables, on-site team size, payment schedule, cancellation terms, and force majeure must be in writing. If the planner resists a written contract, walk away.
