Vendor Sign-Up & KYC Verification: Join the Weddings.io Network

How verified vendors get discovered, booked, and protected on the Weddings.io platform.

6 min read • May 11, 2026 • By the Weddings.io Editorial Team

Every vendor that joins the network goes through KYC verification: business identity, licensing or registration where applicable, portfolio review, and contact validation.
