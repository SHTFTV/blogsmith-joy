Reward Eligibility Rules

Plain English. What counts, what doesn't, and when credit lands in your account.

What counts as a referral

  • Vendor signs up with your unique planner referral link
  • Vendor activates and pays for at least 1 territory slot
  • Vendor remains in good standing (not refunded/charged-back)
  • Vendor is new to Weddings.io (no prior account in last 12 months)
  • Vendor is in a different brand/legal entity than your studio

What doesn't count

  • Self-referrals or your own sub-brands
  • Free-tier accounts that never activate a paid slot
  • Vendors who cancel within 30 days (clawback)
  • Couples or other planners — vendors only
  • Referrals where the vendor used a different signup link

How credit lands

  • Credit posts on day 31 after vendor's first paid slot
  • Equal to 1 month of that vendor's slot price, every month, while they stay subscribed
  • Auto-applied to your Planner subscription & add-ons
  • Surplus rolls forward as account credit (no expiry)
  • Convert surplus to cash payout once balance ≥ $500

Tier multipliers

  • Starter: 1.0× base credit
  • Pro: 1.25× (refer 8 slots = paid off vs 12)
  • Studio: 1.5× + co-marketing slot in Weddings.io newsletter
  • Millionaires Club members stack their volume discount on top

Pricing FAQ

Is the couples free plan really free?

Yes. Every planning tool — budget, guest list, checklists, floor planner, vendor CRM, AI chatbot — is free forever on one device. No card. No login required. Vendors and planners fund the platform.

Why is there a $4.99 cloud tier for couples?

Because cloud sync, backup, and photo storage cost real money to run. We charge what it costs, not what we could extract. Cancel anytime. Free plan never goes away.

Is territory pricing the same everywhere?

Yes. The numbers on this page, in the app, on every vendor signup flow, in the database functions calculate_slot_count / calculate_slot_price, and in src/lib/territoryPricing.ts are the same. The validator above proves it.

How does the Millionaires Club discount stack?

The volume discount applies to your monthly slot subtotal. Add-ons (TALC.tv, EyeSpyR, Backlinks, Visualizer) bill at list price separately, but Diamond and Millionaire tiers receive bonus add-on credits.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly plans cancel at the end of the billing cycle. Territory slots release on next renewal. No long-term contracts anywhere on the platform.

Do you ever show ads?

No. Vendor listings appear because the vendor locked the territory — never because they paid for ad placement.
