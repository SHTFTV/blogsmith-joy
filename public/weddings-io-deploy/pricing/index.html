Pricing FAQ

Do I need to pay to use Weddings.io?

No. Every planning tool is free forever — budget, guest list, checklist, floor planner, CRM, invoice builder, AI chatbot and all 9 culture pages. No login. No credit card. Premium ($9.99/mo) adds cloud sync and photo storage so you can access your plan from any device.

What's the difference between Premium and Pro?

Premium is for one couple planning one wedding. Pro is for wedding planners managing multiple couples — adds a multi-wedding dashboard, white-label client portal, team tools, unlimited photo storage, and 2 TALC.tv content credits per month.

How does the $2 visualizer pricing work?

Pay $2 per AI render. No subscription. Works on the free, Premium and Pro plans equally. Each render generates a realistic venue layout from your prompt using Gemini.

What about vendors — do you charge them?

Yes. Vendors pay a territory lock fee — $10 per 100,000 people in their city per month — for exclusive category placement. That vendor revenue is what funds free planning tools for couples. How territory pricing works →

Do you ever show ads?

No. Not on the free plan, not on Premium, not on Pro. Ever. Vendor listings are shown because the vendor locked the territory, not because they paid for ad placement.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from your account settings. Your data stays accessible in read-only for 30 days, then exports to a downloadable archive.
