About Medieval Castle Weddings in Óbidos

Óbidos is arguably Portugal's most photogenic town: a perfectly preserved medieval castle town where the entire village is enclosed within ancient walls. The castle (now a luxury pousada hotel) can be hired exclusively for South Asian weddings, creating a unique multi-day wedding destination where guests literally stay inside the castle walls. Surreal baraat processions through medieval gates are a once-in-a-lifetime experience.
